### Key Responsibilities
1. **Full-Stack Development**: Develop, enhance, and maintain applications using Python, Node.js, and React.
2. **Database Management**: Write and maintain PL/SQL scripts, stored procedures, and optimize database logic.
3. **SaaS Implementation**: Configure and implement SaaS platforms and custom scripts to align with operational needs.
4. **Technical Architecture**: Build and document cloud-native architectures, ensuring scalability and security.
5. **Cross-Functional Collaboration**: Gather business requirements and translate them into technical specifications with various teams.
6. **Application Deployment**: Manage deployment processes on AWS, including serverless architectures and IAC using Terraform.
7. **System Integration**: Integrate systems using middleware and event-driven architectures.
8. **Troubleshooting**: Quickly diagnose and resolve production issues, participating in on-call rotations as necessary.
9. **CI/CD Practices**: Stay updated with and implement CI/CD best practices, particularly with GitHub Actions.
10. **Documentation**: Maintain comprehensive technical documentation for processes, development, and deployment.
11. **Leverage AI Tools**: Utilize GenAI development tools for code generation, testing, and security analysis.

### Required Skills
#### Hard Skills
- **Programming Languages**: Advanced skills in Python, Node.js, and React.
- **Database Management**: Strong PL/SQL, experience with Snowflake and MongoDB.
- **Containerization**: Proficiency with Docker and Kubernetes.
- **Cloud Services**: Strong knowledge of AWS services including Lambda, S3, and others.
- **Infrastructure as Code**: Familiarity with Terraform.
- **Testing Frameworks**: Experience with pytest and Jest/React Testing Library.
- **Security Practices**: Understanding of application security methods (OWASP Top 10, SAST/DAST).
- **Observability Tools**: Experience with monitoring systems like CloudWatch or Datadog.

#### Soft Skills
- **Communication**: Strong interpersonal skills for effective collaboration.
- **Independent Work**: Ability to work autonomously while effectively being part of a distributed team.
- **Analytical Skills**: Capability to interpret complex processes and translate them into tech solutions.
- **Adaptability**: Comfort working across time zones and adapting to different work cultures.
- **Passion for Technology**: Eagerness to stay updated with emerging technologies and trends.

### Seniority Level
- **Senior Level**: The role necessitates at least 6 years of professional software engineering experience.

- At least 6 years of professional experience in software development.
