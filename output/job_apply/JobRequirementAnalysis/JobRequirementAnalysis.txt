### Job Breakdown for Backend Engineer at Azodha

#### Key Responsibilities
- **Design and Development**: 
  - Design, build, and scale backend services and APIs (REST and GraphQL) using Node.js and TypeScript.
  - Create and maintain data pipelines and data warehousing solutions using tools like Airbyte, dbt, Apache Airflow, or AWS Glue.
  
- **Infrastructure Management**: 
  - Manage and optimize AWS infrastructure, including ECS, EC2, S3, RDS, and Lambda.
  
- **CI/CD Management**: 
  - Own CI/CD pipelines and automated testing to ensure fast and reliable release cycles.
  
- **Data Security**: 
  - Develop secure, compliant systems focusing on handling protected health information (PHI).
  
- **Collaboration**: 
  - Participate in code reviews and share best practices.
  - Collaborate with product, QA, and clinical stakeholders throughout the development lifecycle.
  
- **Production Support**: 
  - Support production stability, including on-call coverage as specified in the contract.

#### Required Skills

**Hard Skills**
- 5+ years of professional experience in backend development with Node.js and TypeScript.
- Strong grasp of PostgreSQL, SQL, and ORMs (e.g., Prisma, TypeORM, Sequelize).
- Hands-on experience with AWS services and infrastructure management.
- Proficiency in data pipeline tools (Airbyte, dbt, Airflow, AWS Glue).
- Familiarity with event-driven architectures and message queuing systems.
- Experience with automated testing frameworks and CI/CD practices.

**Soft Skills**
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
- Ability to work independently and manage one's own schedule effectively.
- Strong problem-solving skills and the ability to deliver against milestones.

#### Seniority Level
- **Mid to Senior-Level**: This position requires significant experience (5+ years), indicating a mid to senior-level candidate.

#### Mandatory vs. Nice-to-Have Qualifications

**Mandatory Qualifications**
- 5+ years of backend development experience with Node.js and TypeScript.
- Experience with PostgreSQL and ORMs.
- Familiarity with AWS services and infrastructure management.
- Strong communication skills.
- Availability for approx. 40 hours/week, with overlap with Eastern Time.
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.

**Nice-to-Have Qualifications**
- Experience in healthcare technology with knowledge of HIPAA compliance and standards (FHIR or HL7).
- Infrastructure as Code experience (Terraform or CloudFormation).
- Container orchestration experience (Docker, Kubernetes).
- Knowledge of disaster recovery and backup strategies in AWS.
- Experience with GCP or Azure.
- Contributions to open-source projects.

#### Suggested Interview Topics
- **Technical Questions**: 
  - Discuss your experience with Node.js and TypeScript—can you walk us through a significant project?
  - Explain your understanding and experience with REST and GraphQL APIs.
  - Describe how you have optimized AWS infrastructure in previous roles.
  - What tools have you used for CI/CD, and what practices do you follow?
  
- **Data Management**: 
  - Discuss any experience you have with data pipelines (Airbyte, dbt, etc.) and data warehousing.
  - How do you ensure data security and compliance, especially when dealing with PHI?

- **Collaboration and Communication**: 
  - Provide an example of how you have collaborated with cross-functional teams.
  - Discuss a time when clear communication made a difference in a project.

- **Problem Solving and Autonomy**: 
  - Describe how you prioritize and manage your tasks independently.
  - Have you ever had to troubleshoot a production issue? How did you handle it?

- **Healthcare Technology Environment**: (If applicable)
  - What experience do you have with healthcare technology standards like HIPAA, FHIR, or HL7?
